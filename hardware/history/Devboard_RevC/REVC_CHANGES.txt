U575 DEVBOARD - REV C  (based on RevB)
=================================================

WHAT'S NEW: ON-BOARD 16-CHANNEL ANALOG MUX
 - U5 CD74HC4067 (LCSC C98457), a 16:1 analog mux.
   * COM -> PC4 (ADC1_IN13 / ADC4_IN22)
   * S0-S3 -> PA7 / PB0 / PB1 / PB2
   * Enable is tied low (always on)
   * 100 nF decoupling (C25)
 - J8 is a 2x10 0.1" (2.54 mm) male header for up to 16 sensor outputs (TMR_OUT1-16).
   * Top row, left to right: GND  2  4  6  8  9  11  13  15  GND
   * Bottom row, left to right: 3V3  1  3  5  7  GND  10  12  14  16   (square pad = pin 1 = 3V3)
   * The numbers are the TMR_OUT channel numbers and are printed on the silkscreen.
   * In ribbon-cable order: pin 1 3V3, pin 2 GND, pins 3-10 TMR_OUT1-8, pin 11 GND,
     pins 12-19 TMR_OUT9-16, pin 20 GND. Each group of 8 signals has a ground wire at both ends.
 - Every input has an RC filter before the mux: 1 kohm series + 10 nF to GND.
   * Corner frequency is about 16 kHz; time constant is 10 us.
   * It removes RF/digital pickup from the wires.
   * It isolates the sensor's op-amp output from cable capacitance.
   * It gives the ADC a stiff source to sample.
   * Traces are 9-16 mm to the mux, over the solid GND plane.
 - J6 (the external mux JST-SH) is still there and unchanged: SIG = PA6, sharing S0-S3 with U5.
   You can run the on-board mux and your breakout together for 32 channels, one ADC channel per mux.
   The full keyboard scales the same way.
 - Removed the off-board mux symbol M1 and its unconnected labels from the schematic.
   ERC is now completely clean.

BOARD
 - Size is 25.85 x 107.2 mm; the tail grew about 25 mm.
   Headers J2/J3, the MCU, and the USB section are exactly as in Rev B.
 - USB pairs are the same as Rev B:
     * no vias
     * all 45-degree bends
     * solid In1 GND underneath
     * about 93 ohm differential; order JLC04161H-3313 with impedance control
 - The +3V3 inner plane clearance is now 0.2 mm, so the plane flows between the J8 pins.

CABLES (off-the-shelf)
 - To your numpad's pin header: female-female Dupont jumper ribbon, e.g. the ELEGOO 120-pc kit.
   Peel off a 20-wire strip and keep it as a flat ribbon, so the GND wires stay next to the signals.
 - If a future sensor board gets a matching 2x10 header: a standard 20-pin (2x10) 2.54 mm
   IDC female-female ribbon cable.

NOISE CHECKLIST
 - Power the sensors from J8's 3V3. That is the same rail as the ADC reference, so supply noise
   cancels (the TMR2615 output is ratiometric).
 - Connect all three J8 GND pins to the sensor board.
 - Keep the ribbon short (under about 15-20 cm) and flat.
 - Run the LED 5 V, LED data and LED ground on separate wires, away from the ribbon.
 - Firmware:
     * after changing S0-S3, wait about 1 us before sampling
     * use a sample time of 20+ ADC cycles
     * enable hardware oversampling (4-16x)
     * calibrate rest and bottom-out per key

CHECKS
 - DRC with schematic parity: 0 errors, 0 unconnected, 0 parity issues.
   Only the 2 old footprint-library warnings remain (Y2, J4).
 - ERC: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
