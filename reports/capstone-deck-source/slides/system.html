2 · The solution delivered

Two boards: a controller and a key matrix

Devboard RevK

STM32U5A5RJT6 controller

  • 160 MHz Arm core, 4 MB flash
  • 16 MHz crystal, USB-C with ESD protection
  • USB high speed on the chip’s built-in PHY
  • 16-channel analog mux for future sensors
  • SWD, UART, BOOT, reset and user buttons

Numpad RevE

4 × 4 test matrix

  • 15 MX switch positions with matrix diodes
  • 15 TMR sensor footprints (not yet populated)
  • 15 SK6812 RGB LEDs
  • Rotary encoder with push switch in the bottom-right slot
  • Joins the devboard through one 2 × 25-pin header
