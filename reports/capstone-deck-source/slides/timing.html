Top: the pin bounces as contacts close and open. Firmware accepts the first low sample as the press, then ignores bounce until the pin has been high for 10 ms. Bottom: the 8K build asks for one USB polling chance every 125 µs instead of every 1 ms. These are design diagrams; actual jitter and latency are not yet measured.
