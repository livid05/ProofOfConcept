Reliability checks

  • 20 taps give exactly 20 characters
  • Hold repeats, release stops
  • Unplug and replug recovers
  • 5 minutes idle, no stray keys

Measurements planned

  • Scan jitter from the firmware gap counters
  • Switch-to-USB latency on an oscilloscope
  • Sensor voltage against key travel
  • Actuation accuracy against a feeler gauge
  • Crosstalk to neighbors, goal under 5%

  • USB rate measured; key latency not yet
  • Met so far: wired USB, open standards, 8K USB rate
