1 · Benefit to society

Tunable input helps more people type comfortably

Accessibility

Light, shallow or deep actuation per key can suit weaker hands, tremor or limited finger range.

Ergonomics

Lighter, shorter presses may reduce finger strain for people who type for hours. Testing this is future work.

Open and repairable

Standard USB HID means no driver or vendor lock-in. Reprogramming a key replaces buying a new keyboard.

These are design goals and expected benefits, not measured results yet.

Be upfront that these are potential benefits. The measurable part today is the open, standard-HID design. Ergonomic claims would need a user study.
