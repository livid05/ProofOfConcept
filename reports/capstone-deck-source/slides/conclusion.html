What went well

  • Custom boards through revision K
  • USB high speed with a working key
  • Saved known-good builds and tests

What I would improve

  • Confirm the exact chip part before choosing tools
  • Test hardware earlier, before adding features
  • Plan for programmer problems with a USB fallback

What I learned

  • Compiling is not the same as working
  • Keep one change per hardware test
  • Write down evidence, not assumptions
