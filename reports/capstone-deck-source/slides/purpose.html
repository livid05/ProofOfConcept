1 · Purpose and motivation

One keyboard cannot be fast, comfortable and portable

Enthusiasts pick a keyboard tuned for one job: competitive gaming, comfortable typing or travel. Every key on a typical mechanical switch fires at one fixed depth, about 2 mm.

Magnetic sensing (Hall effect and tunnel magnetoresistance, TMR) can measure key travel, so the actuation point becomes a software setting. Few products also offer wired and wireless modes, a compact layout and open tools.

Project goal

A compact keyboard where every key has its own programmable actuation distance

Supports mechanical and TMR switches, USB-C wired input first, with wireless as a later stretch goal.
